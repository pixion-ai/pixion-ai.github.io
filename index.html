Solution

Signage beyond displays —
built into the light itself

Pixion devices mount directly onto existing lighting rails and downlights — no new wiring, no separate screens. Cloud-connected software manages content, schedules, and device status in real time.

Information projected exactly where customers walk

Mounted at the lighting above, Pixion devices project high-brightness content onto floors and walls at the precise moment and location it matters most — turning passive spaces into active media.

How it works

From the ceiling, to the path, to the customer

Effective information delivery happens along the customer's path. Pixion uses the position of lighting fixtures — already above every customer at every moment — as the delivery point for contextual media.

01

Install into existing fixtures

Pixion units mount into rail lighting and downlights. No rewiring. No construction. Setup takes minutes, not hours.

02

Configure content by zone

Assign content to each device or zone, set schedules, and define trigger conditions — all from a web or app dashboard, without technical expertise.

03

Project along the customer path

Devices project promotional content, navigation cues, or event information directly onto surfaces in front of customers as they move through the space.

04

Measure and optimize

Pedestrian response and guidance data is aggregated automatically, so you can see how each campaign performs and adjust content and time slots accordingly.
